WHO WE ARE

A product company built around real needs, not trends.

Swed Technology Ltd was established in Israel in 2024 as a private company based on a simple insight: entire industries still run on paper, spreadsheets, and phone calls. We find those markets, understand how they actually work, and build digital platforms that fit.

The company is led by a product engineer with years of experience in financial systems, insurance platforms, UX, and complex systems development—a background that shapes how we build: documented, tested, secure, and maintained for years after launch.

OUR MISSION

To turn traditional, manual processes into simple digital products that people actually want to use.
